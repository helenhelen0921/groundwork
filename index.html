Shuowen Jiezi entries for other characters come from the shuowen.org data set, used under the Apache License 2.0; simplified characters were matched to traditional forms with OpenCC.

Quotations from the Shuowen Jiezi and classical texts are in the public domain. The layer stories for the excavation sites are curated by Helen. Only the Shuowen (c. AD 100) and oracle bone (c. 1200 BC) layers carry dates; the others are approximate periods.
